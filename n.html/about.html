About JalSahay 💧

JalSahay is a digital rural water complaint and maintenance management platform designed to improve communication between citizens and water authorities.

🎯 Our Purpose

Rural communities may experience problems such as pipeline leakage, water contamination, irregular supply and damaged water infrastructure. JalSahay provides a centralized digital platform through which residents can report these problems and track their progress.

⚙️ How JalSahay Works

A citizen submits a complaint through the online complaint form. The system generates a unique complaint ID. The complaint can then be tracked by the citizen, while administrators can view complaints and update their status.

📋 Report

Citizens can report water-related problems through an easy-to-use form.

🔎 Track

Every complaint receives a unique ID for tracking its progress.

📊 Analyze

Authorities can analyze complaint patterns using analytics dashboards.

🚀 Future Vision

The platform can be expanded with GPS-based location reporting, image uploads, email authentication, automated notifications, water-quality monitoring, advanced analytics and centralized municipal dashboards.
